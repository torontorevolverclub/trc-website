Firearm Safety → 02 · On the firing line

Range Safety

How we shoot safely together.

  • Range Officer on the line at all times
  • Know the range commands
  • Anyone can call cease fire
  • Eye and ear protection when on the range
Range Safety → 03 · At TRC

Club Practices

How things work at our club.

  • Use the secured contactless entry system to log club entry
  • Cased firearms in the clubhouse
  • No ammo at the Inspection Table
  • .22 rimfire rifles only
  • Guests supervised within arm’s reach
