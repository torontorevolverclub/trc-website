Becoming a Member

The Toronto Revolver Club welcomes people interested in target pistol sports. Joining takes a few steps, so every new member starts out safe, confident and comfortable on our range.

Shooting experience and pistol ownership are welcome, but not required.

New members

How it works

  1. 1

    Contact us

    Get in touch with the Membership Secretary and tell us you’re interested. Questions are welcome at any stage.

  2. 2

    Tell us a little about yourself

    Fill in a short questionnaire (about 10 minutes).

    Start the questionnaire →
  3. 3

    Visit the club

    Come to an information session, meet our members, see the range, and find out what pistol shooting is all about.

  4. 4

    Learn the basics, safely

    Our safety course covers everything from the ground up: how to handle a pistol, how the range works, and what to expect on the firing line.

  5. 5

    Shoot with a mentor

    Your first live-fire sessions are with an experienced club mentor beside you, at your own pace.

  6. 6

    Welcome to the club

    Prospective members who have demonstrated to their mentors and members of the TRC Board that they meet the club’s knowledge and safety standards may be approved as members by the Board.
