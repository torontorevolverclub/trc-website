120+ Years of Tradition

Formed in 1905 and in continuous operation ever since, the Toronto Revolver Club is the oldest sport pistol club in Canada and one of the oldest in North America.

Founders & legends

Club founder · 1905

Alex Rutherford

A decorated Boer War veteran, Alex Rutherford founded the Toronto Revolver Club in 1905 to cultivate disciplined pistol marksmanship and civic sporting fellowship in Toronto.

Travelling team legacy

Len Appleyard

A De Havilland Aircraft Company employee and medalist on the TRC travelling team, he represented the club across Ontario, Michigan and New York, bringing home championship honours.
