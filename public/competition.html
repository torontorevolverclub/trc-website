Programs & Leagues · tap to open

Club Matches

Club MatchesTue & Wed

Every Tuesday morning and Wednesday night. Tuesday morning matches are ISSF 600 matches, shot under ISSF rules and recognized by the Shooting Federation of Canada. Wednesday night matches combine ISSF and NRA-style events; the ISSF events are also SFC-recognized.

The Bill Wood ISSF 1200 Matches

Bill Wood 1200ISSF 1200

Named in honour of TRC’s own Olympian and longtime team captain Bill Wood, these monthly matches follow International Shooting Sport Federation (ISSF) rules. Shooters compete across precision and rapid-fire stages, making this an ideal training ground for athletes preparing for provincial and national championships.

Metro Pistol League (MPL)

MPL2 Teams

TRC fields two teams in this longstanding Greater Toronto Area inter-club league. Matches are shot in the classic “Bullseye” style (one-handed and unsupported) using both .22 LR rimfire and centrefire handguns. It is a welcoming, handicap-accessible format that allows newer competitors to shoot alongside seasoned marksmen.

Metro Free Pistol League

Free PistolLeague

Often considered the purest test of marksmanship, “Free Pistol” is a classic international discipline shot one-handed with specialized single-shot .22 LR target pistols. Matches emphasize extreme precision, patience and trigger control.

10-Metre Air Pistol

Air Pistol10 M

Using .177 calibre compressed-air pistols, this Olympic discipline is one of the most accessible entry points into competitive target shooting. It requires minimal equipment, has virtually no recoil, and builds the exact foundational mechanics needed for all other ISSF pistol events.

IPSC

IPSCPractical

A small group of TRC members who have earned their IPSC Black Badge shoot a practical-shooting match about once a month. The Black Badge qualification is required before taking part.

Pathway to Provincial & International Competition

From Club Line to the Podium

Target shooting is an inclusive, lifelong Olympic and Paralympic sport open to athletes of all ages and physical abilities. The disciplines practised weekly at the Toronto Revolver Club mirror the standards governed provincially, nationally and internationally. Members looking to test their skills beyond the club can progress through sanctioned competitions hosted by our sport’s governing bodies.
