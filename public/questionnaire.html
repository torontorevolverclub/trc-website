Applicant Questionnaire

TRC Target Sports Program. Tell us a little about yourself. It takes about 10 minutes, and your answers go by email to the club’s President and Membership Secretary.

1About you

Weekly club nights and scheduled 1-on-1 training sessions take place in East York. Can you attend consistently for the full 12-month program? *

2Licensing & costs

The program starts with mentor-supplied firearms and requires progressive licensing under Canadian law. The club can’t guarantee that you’ll be able to buy your own pistol.

Do you currently hold a Canadian PAL or Restricted PAL (RPAL)? *

Main costs over the 12 months

  • About $450Canadian Restricted Firearms Safety Course (RPAL) and exam fees
  • $60012-month TRC Target Sports Program fee
  • $600 – $4,000Your own Olympic-style target pistol (later, optional)

Are you prepared to meet these costs on schedule? *

3Goals & learning

Are you interested in training toward, and competing in, Olympic-style (ISSF) pistol events? *

4Open house & confirmation

Your privacy: answers are not stored on this website. They are sent by email (through the FormSubmit form service) to the club’s President and Membership Secretary, used only for applicant selection, and not shared outside the club.

Thank you!

Your answers have been sent to the club. The Membership Secretary will be in touch about next steps.
