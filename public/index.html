INSTITUTIONAL LINEAGE // 1905 – PRESENT

120+ YEARS OF TRADITION

Formed in 1905 and in continuous operation ever since, the Toronto Revolver Club is the oldest sport pistol club in Canada and one of the oldest in North America.

CLUB FOUNDER • 1905

Alex Rutherford

Boer War Veteran • Founded TRC in 1905 • d. April 18, 1934 (Age 58)

A decorated Boer War veteran, Alex Rutherford founded the Toronto Revolver Club in 1905 to cultivate disciplined pistol marksmanship and civic sporting fellowship in Toronto. His leadership established the enduring standards of sportsmanship and precision that continue to define TRC more than 120 years later.

TRAVELLING TEAM LEGACY

Len Appleyard

De Havilland Aircraft Company • Travelling Team Medalist

A dedicated De Havilland Aircraft Company employee and decorated medalist on the renowned TRC travelling shooting team, Len Appleyard represented the club across Ontario, Michigan, and New York—bringing home championship honours and cementing TRC’s competitive reputation.
